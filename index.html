Rulebook

How I play

Four rules I design by, in the order I use them.

  1. 1

    State the intent

    Every wave, flag and role starts as one sentence about what the player should notice or decide.

  2. 2

    Put it in a table

    Segments, enemies and tactics live in rows I can sort, compare and argue about.

  3. 3

    Build it

    A rule isn't finished until it runs in a Unity or Ren'Py build.

  4. 4

    Tune it

    Check the build against the intent from rule 1, then retune until the two match.
